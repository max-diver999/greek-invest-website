## How Many Greek Golden Visas Are Active Right Now?

The current stock is 27,786 valid main-investor permits as of December 2025, the largest of any European residence-by-investment programme, up roughly 47% in two years. Family permits multiply the headcount by about 2.3 times on pending-file ratios, and the stock keeps climbing because approvals are draining a deep backlog.

| Stock measure | Figure | As of |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active main-investor permits | 27,786 (100%) | December 2025 |
| Initial active holders, China | 8,179 (~47% of initial) | Late 2025 |
| Initial active holders, Turkey | 2,499 (+158% in 12 months) | Late 2025 |
| Cumulative US approvals | 578 (+49% in 12 months) | December 2025 |
| Pending files, all categories | 49,671 (70% family members) | June 2025 |
| Pending investor applications | 14,931 (83% initial filings) | June 2025 |

Two opposite readings of the stock are both correct:

- As scale: the asset class is institutionalised, processing is routine.
- As overhang: queued files are tomorrow's supply of resale stock.

Greece now runs Europe's largest residence-by-investment programme by permits in force, and a buyer should hold both readings simultaneously. Read as programme scale, 27,786 active investor permits validate the asset class: banks, notaries and the Ministry process these files as routine, and the resale market for compliant stock has a decade of comparables. Read as market overhang, the nearly 15,000 investor files still queued in mid-2025 are demand already banked, feeding approvals of roughly 950 permits a month for 24 months through 2026 and 2027 regardless of what new applications do, and every one of those approvals eventually becomes a holder who may sell. The stock figure is therefore both the programme's credibility and its future supply curve, which is why serious buyers track it quarterly rather than quoting it once.

## How Have Applications and Approvals Moved Since 2024?

The headline fact of 2025 is a crossover: filings fell 25.7% from their record of roughly 9,300 down to 6,978, while approvals rose 95% to 8,879, and the divergence widened in early 2026, applications down a further 43% and approvals up 11% at 3,816 for January to April.

| Flow measure | 2024 | 2025 | Jan-Apr 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial applications | ~9,300 (record) | 6,978 (−25.7%) | −43% year on year |
| Approvals, main investors | ~4,550 | 8,879 (+95%) | 3,816 (+11%) |
| Monthly application pace | 774 avg; 1,048 in Q4 | ~580 | falling |
| Monthly approval pace | ~380 | ~740 | ~950 |

The mechanics behind the cross are straightforward:

- The 2024 application record was a deadline rush around Law 5100/2024's threshold rises.
- The €250,000 studio buyer, most of the old volume, left when the floor moved to €400,000.
- The Ministry scaled processing, cutting the backlog roughly 10% in a single quarter after June 2025.
- Approvals now track the queue, not the market: 2026's grants are mostly 2024's files.

A buyer reading the flow table should invert the panic headlines: falling applications mean fewer competitors at the €400,000 tier, while rising approvals mean the [8-to-14-month queue](/guides/greece-golden-visa-timeline-application-2026/) is shortening, both favourable to a 2026 entrant.

## Which Nationalities Hold and Seek Greek Golden Visas?

China dominates the stock with 8,179 initial holders, about 47% of the total, but all of the growth is elsewhere: Turkey added 1,529 holders in a single year for +158%, US cumulative approvals rose 49% to 578, and Israeli and UK demand climbed on regional and post-Brexit logics respectively.

| Nationality | Holders / approvals | Change | Share of pending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| China | 8,179 initial holders | steady | 56.4% of pending investor files |
| Turkey | 2,499 initial holders | +158% in one year | 16.3% of pending |
| United States | 578 cumulative approvals | +49% year on year | rising |
| Israel, UK, Gulf | growing cohorts | double-digit growth | single digits each |

Nationality composition explains programme behaviour better than any other variable in the dataset. Chinese demand, 56.4% of pending investor files, is stock-heavy and queue-patient, filed largely in the €250,000 era and still working through the system years later. Turkish demand is proximity-driven and current, flowing into Thessaloniki, Halkidiki and Athens at a +158% annual growth rate, and it is the cohort actively repricing the [€400,000 regional tier](/guides/crete-golden-visa-400000-property/). American demand is the smallest of the three at 578 cumulative approvals but compounds fastest in percentage terms at +49% a year, fed by both the non-dom relocation logic and remote work. For an owner planning an exit, the mix is a liquidity forecast: today's pending files are tomorrow's buyers of compliant 120 m² stock, and their nationalities tell you which submarkets they will bid in.

## What Do the Numbers Say About Money and the Market?

Foreign real estate inflows into Greece fell 25.3% in 2025 while Golden Visa approvals nearly doubled, which reads as a contradiction and is actually composition: fewer, larger deals replaced many small ones after thresholds tripled from €250,000, and domestic buyers held national transaction volumes steady through the shift.

The money picture in four dated facts:

- Foreign real estate inflows: −25.3% in 2025, per Bank of Greece reporting, the first sustained fall of the programme era.
- Athens transactions: 5,816 transfers worth €626 million in 2025, an average near €107,630, 6.8% above the national mean.
- Minimum ticket at the prime tier: €800,000, implying €856,000 to €880,000 all-in with the 3.09% transfer tax and fees.
- Programme-era house prices: Athens prime and Riviera stock repriced upward through 2024 to 2026 even as inflow euros fell, scarcity of compliant 120 m² stock doing the work.

The investment-side conclusion is not that demand died; it is that the programme moved upmarket exactly as designed, trading volume for ticket size, with [national price data](/guides/greece-property-market-forecast-2026-2027/) showing the two-speed market that resulted.

Every inflection in the data is a rule change wearing a chart: the 2013 launch at €250,000, the 2023 doubling to €500,000 in Athens and Thessaloniki, and Law 5100/2024's three-tier reset at €800,000, €400,000 and €250,000 with the 120 m² floor and the short-term rental ban attached.

## How Many Family Members Ride on Each Investor Permit?

The family multiplier is about 2.3: June 2025's queue held 34,740 family-member files against 14,931 investor files, because one Greek deed covers a spouse, children under 21 and the parents of both spouses at no extra investment. Renewals were 17% of pending investor applications, the first 2019-era five-year cohorts coming due.

- One investor file typically brings 2 to 3 dependants into the queue.
- Parents of both spouses are the uniquely Greek inclusion, and visibly used.
- Renewal volume will compound from 2026 as the post-2019 stock cycles through its five-year terms.

For programme arithmetic this means headline "permit" counts triple when families are counted, and Ministry capacity is consumed accordingly, one reason [processing runs 8 to 14 months](/guides/greece-golden-visa-timeline-application-2026/) even at record approval pace. The [family rules guide](/guides/greece-golden-visa-family-members-rules/) prices the inclusion itself.

## What Are the Advantages and Disadvantages of Entering at 2026's Numbers?

The advantages of a 2026 entry are a thinner field and a faster queue: applications are down 43%, approvals up 11%, and the backlog is shrinking by about 10% a quarter. The disadvantages are price and scarcity: the ticket tripled against 2022, compliant 120 m² stock is scarce, and the easy-yield era is legislated away.

**Advantages of entering now**

- Fewer competing buyers: filings are at their lowest since 2017 outside the pandemic year.
- Faster decisions: approval pace near 950 a month against ~380 in 2024.
- Seller leverage at €400,000: foreign inflows −25.3% means negotiable vendors.
- Rule clarity: Circular 1/2026 replaced discretion with checklists.

**Disadvantages of entering now**

- Capital: €430,000 to €880,000 all-in against €265,000 in the €250,000 era.
- Compliant stock scarcity: 120 m² single-deed supply is the binding constraint in prime zones.
- Income limits: the STR ban plus 15% to 45% rental tax cap net yields near 2% to 3%.
- Backlog risk in reverse: 15,000 queued investor files are also 15,000 future exit-sellers.

## What Are the Risks and Red Flags in Reading These Statistics?

The dataset's main risks are definitional rather than arithmetical: mixing main-applicant counts with family-inclusive counts that run 2.3 times larger, quoting stock as flow, and trusting undated figures from the €250,000 era. Four reading rules keep the numbers honest, and they are the rules this page is built on.

- **Main investors vs everyone.** 27,786 is investors only; pending-file ratios imply the all-permits figure is more than double. A source that switches base mid-argument is selling something.
- **Stock vs flow.** "Record Golden Visas" can mean permits in force, annual approvals or annual filings; in 2025 those three numbers were 27,786, 8,879 and 6,978 respectively.
- **Dates or it did not happen.** Thresholds changed in 2023 and 2024; any statistic without a period attached is unusable for decisions.
- **Source chain.** Primary data is the Ministry of Migration's quarterly releases; everything else, this page included, is a relay and says so.
