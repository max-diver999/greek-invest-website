**Quick answer:** the Greek purchase process did not change in 2026. The numbers
attached to it did. The rental income scale moved on 1 January, the Ministry issued
Circular 1/2026 on how the Golden Visa thresholds are applied, the new-build VAT
position continued to differ from the resale position, and Athens tightened
short-term rental rules. This page is the dated log, with each item linking to the
guide that holds it in full.

## Why this page is a log rather than an article

Search demand around Greek property news is largely a recency question: people want
to know whether what they read last month still holds. A single explanatory article
cannot answer that, and a site that keeps rewriting one page loses the record of what
changed and when.

So this is a log. Each entry says what moved, when, and which guide carries the
detail. Nothing here restates a guide at length, because duplicating them would make
both worse.

## 1 January 2026, the rental income scale

Law 5246/2025 changed the bands an individual landlord pays on gross rent, and
inserted a new band in the middle of the range. Income that previously sat in a lower
band moved up. The deduction position did not change and remains the harsher half:
only a flat allowance for repairs may be set against gross rent, with nothing for
management, insurance or finance costs.

Who this affects: anyone letting a Greek property as an individual, whether resident
or not. Anyone whose yield model was built before 2026 is working from the wrong
scale.

Detail: [the 2026 tax changes under Law 5246](/guides/greece-tax-changes-2026-law-5246/)
and [rental income tax for non-residents](/guides/greece-rental-income-tax-non-resident/).

## 22 April 2026, Circular 1/2026 on the Golden Visa

The Ministry of Migration and Asylum published the circular that operationalises the
thresholds set by Law 5100/2024. The law had set the tiers; the circular set out how
they are applied in practice, including the treatment of the single-property and
usable-area conditions and the money-movement requirements.

Who this affects: anyone with a Golden Visa file in progress, and anyone shortlisting
property against a threshold. The circular is the document a Ministry officer reads,
which makes it more operationally important than the law it implements.

Detail: [Circular 1/2026 explained](/guides/greece-golden-visa-circular-2026-explained/)
and [the tier structure](/guides/greece-golden-visa-property-tiers-2026/).

## Through 2026, new-build VAT

The suspension affecting VAT on first sales by developers continued to shape how a
new-build purchase is taxed compared with a resale. The two routes are taxed under
different regimes, and on the same headline price that produces materially different
sums.

Who this affects: anyone comparing an off-plan or newly completed property against an
equivalent resale. The tax question belongs in the comparison before a reservation
rather than at the notary.

Detail: [the new-build VAT suspension](/guides/greece-new-build-vat-suspension-2026/).

## Through 2026, short-term rental restrictions in Athens

Restrictions on new short-term rental registrations in parts of central Athens
tightened. This sits alongside the separate and unrelated rule that a Golden Visa
qualifying property may not be let short-term at all, for the life of the permit.

Who this affects: anyone buying in central Athens with a short-let model in mind, and
separately anyone whose purchase doubles as a residence application.

Detail: [the Athens short-term rental moratorium](/guides/athens-short-term-rental-moratorium-2026/).

## What did not change

Worth stating, because it is the part guides get right for years and readers assume
is volatile.

The purchase mechanics are the same: a Greek tax number and a bank account first, a
lawyer acting for you and a notary acting for neither party, a civil engineer
certifying the building's legality, a notarial preliminary agreement, a final deed
and registration at the cadastre. Foreign buyers still hold the same freehold
interest as Greek nationals, and border zone consent still applies to non-EU buyers
in designated areas.

If a guide describes that sequence correctly, its age does not make it wrong. If it
quotes a rate, a band or a threshold, check the date on it.

## Where to check for yourself

| Question | Primary source |
|---|---|
| What a law says | Government Gazette |
| How the Ministry applies a Golden Visa rule | Ministry of Migration and Asylum circulars |
| What transaction prices did | Bank of Greece residential price indices, quarterly |
| What sellers are asking now | Commercial property portals, a different measure |

The last row deserves the caution. Portal averages are asking prices from live
listings and central bank indices measure recorded transactions. Both are useful and
they answer different questions, which is why a figure quoted without its source
status is worth very little.
