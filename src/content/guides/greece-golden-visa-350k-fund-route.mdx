**Quick answer:** Subscribe €350,000 or more to a fund investing exclusively in Greek-listed assets, with AUM of at least €10 million, and the subscription carries a full Golden Visa: the programme's lowest ticket, investment-complete in 2 to 4 weeks, at running fees of 1% to 2% a year and with no property to manage.

Since Law 5100/2024 doubled the property tiers and left the financial floors alone, the fund route has been the cheapest door into the Greek programme, €50,000 under the [€400,000 regional tier](/guides/greece-golden-visa-property-tiers-2026/), and it remains the least documented in English. The [overview of all five non-property routes](/guides/greece-golden-visa-without-property/) seats it in the menu; this guide runs the route end to end.

Everything below is current as of 22 August 2026: what makes a fund eligible, the four-week execution sequence, the fee arithmetic over a five-year permit cycle, what renewals actually inspect, the diligence that separates an investment from a visa wrapper, and who should still buy property instead.

## What Exactly Qualifies as a Golden Visa Fund?

An eligible vehicle is a UCITS mutual fund or an Alternative Investment Fund whose mandate is exclusively shares, corporate bonds or government bonds listed on Greek regulated markets or multilateral trading facilities, with total fund assets of at least €10 million; your own subscription must be at least €350,000 of that vehicle.

- Vehicle: UCITS or AIF. - Mandate: Greek-listed only. - Scale: €10M AUM. - Ticket: €350,000.

| Test | Requirement | Evidence in the file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vehicle type | UCITS mutual fund or AIF | Fund rules / prospectus |
| Mandate | Exclusively Greek-listed shares and bonds | Investment policy pages |
| Scale | Fund AUM ≥ €10 million | Manager's certificate |
| Ticket | ≥ €350,000 subscription | Subscription confirmation |
| Custody | Units held for the applicant | Registry and custody statements |

Three boundaries decide eligibility in practice, and each has failed real files. The mandate test is exclusive: a fund holding 15% of its book in non-Greek paper fails it outright, and mandate drift after subscription is a renewal problem rather than merely an investment one, because the holding you evidence in year five must still pass the same test it passed at grant. The fund's domicile is flexible, Greece or abroad both work, so long as the exclusively-Greek-listed mandate and the €10 million AUM floor hold, which is why Luxembourg wrappers over Athens Exchange portfolios appear alongside Greek-domiciled vehicles. And the taxonomy has been refined more than once since Law 5100/2024, so a current-year, written eligibility confirmation naming the exact share class, from both the manager and independent counsel, is the document that opens every serious file and the absence that should close one.

## How Does the Four-Week Execution Sequence Run?

The route is investment-complete in roughly four weeks: a Greek bank relationship and KYC in weeks one and two, the €350,000 wire and the subscription in week three, certificates in week four, after which the immigration file lodges and joins the same 8-to-14-month Ministry queue as every property applicant.

1. Week 1 to 2: open the Greek bank relationship; pass KYC; this step, not the fund, sets the calendar.
2. Week 2 to 3: wire €350,000+ from an account in your own name, the [Circular 1/2026 trail](/guides/golden-visa-greece-bank-transfer-proof-funds/) documented end to end.
3. Week 3: execute the subscription at the fund's next dealing date; AIFs may deal monthly, so the dealing calendar is a real variable.
4. Week 4: collect the migration-format certificates, subscription, registry entry, custody, AUM and mandate confirmations.
5. Lodge the Ministry file; biometrics; the [standard queue](/guides/greece-golden-visa-timeline-application-2026/) runs from here.

Against a deed's 10 to 14 weeks the route saves roughly two months at the front, which is decisive for applicants racing a school year, a passport expiry or a 31 March tax-election deadline in the [combined non-dom plan](/guides/greece-golden-visa-plus-non-dom-strategy/), and irrelevant to everyone else, because the Ministry end is identical.

## What Does the Route Cost Over a Five-Year Cycle?

The realistic budget is €17,500 to €35,000 of fees over the first five-year permit: management and administration of 1% to 2% a year on the €350,000, plus any subscription charge at entry, set against whatever Greek listed markets return, and against zero rental income by construction of the route.

| Cost line | Typical range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Management + admin fees | 1% to 2% a year | €3,500 to €7,000 annually |
| Subscription charge | 0% to 2% one-off | Negotiable at this ticket |
| Custody and certificates | Hundreds per year | Migration-format documents included |
| Legal, both files | €4,000 to €8,000 | Eligibility opinion plus immigration |
| Government fees | €2,000/adult + €150/member | Same as property route |
| Redemption terms at exit | Fund-specific | Read before subscribing, not after |

The honest comparison with property is not fees but opportunity cost, and it can be counted. A [€400,000 regional apartment](/guides/greece-golden-visa-property-tiers-2026/) grosses 4% to 6% in rent, roughly €16,000 to €24,000 a year before Greek rental tax, while the fund charges 1% to 2%, €3,500 to €7,000, to hold Greek listed beta the applicant could buy without any visa attached. Across a five-year permit cycle that spread is €100,000 or more of forgone net income against fees paid, before either asset's price movement is considered. The route's economics therefore only win for applicants who value zero operations above yield, no tenant, no ENFIA, no engineer, no August phone calls, which is a legitimate preference, precisely priced, and the route's entire honest market.

## What Does Renewal Actually Inspect on a Fund File?

The five-year renewal inspects exactly one thing: an unbroken qualifying holding of at least €350,000 by original subscription value, evidenced by fresh registry and custody certificates and the documented chain since grant. Every fund-side corporate action, a redemption, merger, liquidation or mandate change, is therefore also a residence-permit event.

- **Redemption without replacement** ends eligibility, exactly as selling the Golden Visa flat would; replacement into another qualifying vehicle must be gapless and documented.
- **Fund mergers and liquidations** happen to niche vehicles; the successor fund must itself pass the mandate and AUM tests, in writing.
- **Mandate drift** off exclusively-Greek assets disqualifies silently; an annual letter from the manager confirming the mandate belongs in your renewal file.
- **Market value falls** below €350,000 do not by themselves break the rule, which is tested against the subscription, but counsel should document the position at each renewal.

The discipline is diary work: every dealing date, report date and corporate-action notice for 15+ years of intended holding. Property owners have ENFIA and tenants; fund holders have the manager's calendar. Neither route is maintenance-free, they differ in what needs maintaining.

## How Do You Diligence the Fund Itself?

The right order is to judge the vehicle as an investment first and a permit second: manager track record, total cost, liquidity terms and governance, because a fund good enough to hold €350,000 for five to fifteen years is a materially higher bar than a fund merely eligible for a visa.

- **Manager:** years running Greek-mandate money, team depth, and assets beyond visa-driven inflows; a fund that is 90% Golden Visa money has redemption dynamics tied to immigration policy, not markets.
- **Cost:** total expense ratio, not headline management fee; 3%+ all-in vehicles exist precisely because visa demand is price-insensitive.
- **Liquidity:** dealing frequency, notice periods, gates and side letters; you may hold through two renewals, but exits happen, and their terms are set now.
- **Governance:** depositary, auditor, regulator and jurisdiction; a Luxembourg or Athens-regulated wrapper with a name-brand depositary reads differently from an exotic one.
- **Paper:** the eligibility opinion, the mandate confirmation and the AUM certificate, dated, signed and renewed annually.

None of this is exotic; it is ordinary fund diligence applied by people who are usually property buyers. The failure pattern to avoid is inverted priorities: choosing the fund for its visa paperwork and discovering the investment inside it afterwards.

## How Does the Fund Route Combine With the Non-Dom Election?

The fund route pairs naturally with Greece's flat-tax regimes, because Greek securities are themselves a qualifying 5A investment category: a subscription that carries the visa can be topped up from €350,000 to €500,000 and carry the [€100,000 non-dom election](/guides/greece-non-dom-tax-regime-5a-guide/) too, all without a single square metre of property.

- **The €150,000 top-up.** 5A requires €500,000 of Greek investment; the visa needs €350,000. One fund position at €500,000 satisfies both tests in one custody account, the fully liquid version of the €800,000 Riviera deed strategy.
- **The sequencing is identical.** Visa first on the subscription; the 5A election only in the year you genuinely relocate, filed by 31 March with the same Greek-bank evidence the subscription already produced.
- **What the flat tax then shelters.** Foreign income worldwide at €100,000 a year; the Greek fund's own distributions are Greek-source and taxed under ordinary Greek rules, a mirror of the rent on a Golden Visa flat.
- **Pensioners skip the top-up.** The [7% regime](/guides/greece-7-percent-pension-tax-retirees/) has no investment test at all, so a retiree can hold the €350,000 fund for the visa and elect 7% on everything foreign with nothing further invested.

For a mobile investor who may or may not ever move to Greece, the combined position costs €500,000 of Greek listed exposure and holds every option open at once: residence now with zero stay required, a 15-year tax shield exercisable in any later year by one form filed before 31 March, and an exit that is a redemption order rather than a months-long property sale campaign.

## What Are the Advantages and Disadvantages of the Fund Route?

The route's advantages are price, speed and cleanliness: €350,000 of entry, four weeks to invest, no tenant, no ENFIA, no 120 m² geometry to certify. The disadvantages are negative running economics against a let property, counterparty discretion over your qualifying asset, and an eligibility taxonomy that moves more often than property law does.

**Advantages**

- Lowest ticket in the programme: €50,000 under the cheapest property tier.
- Investment-complete in 2 to 4 weeks; no notary, engineer or cadastre.
- Zero operational Greece: nothing to insure, let, maintain or measure.
- Family inclusion identical to property, both sets of parents included.
- Succession passes units, not Greek conveyancing.

**Disadvantages**

- Fees of 1% to 2% a year against a property's 4% to 6% gross rent.
- Manager and fund-structure risk sit inside your residence status.
- Niche Greek-mandate vehicles: thin track records, visa-driven flows.
- Eligibility rules refined since 2024; files age faster than property files.
- Nothing to use: the route buys mobility, not a Greek address.

## What Are the Risks and Red Flags Before Subscribing?

Four red flags decide most bad fund files: an eligibility opinion nobody put in writing, total annual costs above 2.5%, dealing terms that trap the exit behind long notice, and a fund whose only investors are visa applicants. Each flag is visible before subscription to anyone who asks two questions.

- **No written eligibility confirmation** from manager and counsel, current-year, naming the exact share class: walk.
- **All-in cost above ~2.5%:** the visa is subsidising the manager; cheaper eligible vehicles exist.
- **Quarterly-or-worse dealing with long notice:** fine to hold, punitive to leave; read the exit before the entry.
- **Investor base = visa applicants:** redemption waves follow policy news; ask for the split.
- **Trail defects:** third-party payers, mismatched names or non-bank transfers fail Circular 1/2026 on funds exactly as on deeds.

## Which Applicant Scenario Fits the €350K Fund Route?

The route is built for three applicants: the mobility-first case with no Greek life planned, the deadline case needing investment completion inside 4 weeks, and the diversified investor adding a permit to a portfolio line. It does not fit relocating families, yield seekers, or anyone who wants keys.

- Mobility only: fund. - Deadline: fund. - Living or letting: property.

**The mobility-first applicant.** Schengen access is the product; Greece stays on paper. €350,000 in a clean vehicle, certificates in a month, and no further Greek surface for five years at a time.

**The deadline applicant.** A 31 March non-dom election or a September school place that a 14-week purchase would miss: the fund closes the gap, and the [timeline mathematics](/guides/greece-golden-visa-timeline-application-2026/) do the rest.

**The relocating family.** Buy the property instead: €50,000 more at the [€400,000 tier](/guides/greece-golden-visa-property-tiers-2026/) returns a home and rent, and the fund's advantages evaporate the day you need somewhere to live.
