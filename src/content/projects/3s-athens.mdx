## A 2027 handover means you are buying under rules that will not be the rules

3S Athens is marketed for delivery in 2027. Among the schemes reviewed on this site that is the longest
gap between a reservation and a deed, and length is not a neutral quantity in Greek property. It is the
scheme's principal characteristic.

Greek residence and tax rules attached to property have moved repeatedly and they move with dates on
them. The Golden Visa thresholds themselves were rewritten in 2024. The suspension of VAT on new
residential deliveries and the suspension of capital gains tax on disposals both carry stated end dates
that fall before a 2027 completion. And the rule that governs any of these is the one in force on the
day the notarial deed is signed, not the day the reservation was paid.

A buyer reserving in 2026 for a 2027 deed is therefore accepting something more specific than
construction risk: the terms of the transaction are not fixed at the point the money starts moving. The
[new-build VAT position](/guides/greece-new-build-vat-suspension-2026/) explains which charge attaches
to a first delivery and when, and it should be read as a schedule rather than as background.

## Three years is long enough for the counterparty to change

Greca Developments is the counterparty for the whole of that period, and off-plan in Greece means
unsecured exposure to it unless the contract says otherwise. There is no statutory deposit protection
scheme; what protects instalments is whatever the preliminary agreement provides.

Over a three-year build the questions to ask about a developer are different from the ones that matter
on a scheme handing over in months:

| Short build | Three-year build |
|---|---|
| Will the finish match the render? | Will the entity that signed still exist at completion? |
| Is the permit issued? | Does the permit expire, and what happens if it is revised mid-build |
| When is handover? | What triggers each instalment, and can they be re-sequenced |
| Is there a delay penalty? | Is the penalty capped, and does it survive an extension agreed with other buyers |

Check the GEMI filing for the entity actually named in the contract, which on Greek schemes is often a
special-purpose company rather than the developer's trading name. Ask whether the site is owned by that
entity outright and what is registered against it. Neither question is answerable from a brochure and
both are answerable from public registries.

The [developer page for Greca](/developers/greca-developments/) sets out what is publicly known about
the firm's wider activity.

## Twenty units in central Athens is a resale question, not a lifestyle one

Central Athens is the deepest resale market in Greece, and that is the strongest argument for buying
here rather than on the Riviera or an island. A twenty-unit scheme in the centre exits into a market
with year-round buyers, domestic as well as foreign, and does not depend on a single narrow pool.

The qualification is that a Golden Visa exit is not an ordinary exit. Selling the qualifying asset ends
the basis of the permit unless the holder has already naturalised or moved to another status, so the
resale liquidity that makes central Athens attractive is liquidity the permit holder may not be free to
use for years. That is a planning point rather than a defect, and it belongs in the model at purchase:
the realistic hold is long, and the building's running costs over that hold matter more than its
headline resale comparables.

## What Greca does not publish, and what has to replace it

Greca does not publish unified price lists for every 3S Athens stack. As with most central boutique
schemes, that means neither Golden Visa test, €800,000 on one deed, 120 square metres of certified main
usable area, can be checked from public material. Both have to be obtained per unit and in writing.

Central Athens stock prices above outer Attica per square metre, so the common pattern here is a unit
that clears the price test comfortably and sits close to the size line. That is the reverse of the
suburban problem and it needs the same document to resolve: an engineer's certificate stating enclosed
area referenced to the Electronic Building Identity, with balconies and storage itemised separately. The
[120 m² rule](/guides/greece-golden-visa-120-square-meter-rule/) sets out exactly what is countable.

## The specific clauses that matter on a long build

1. **Instalments tied to certified structural milestones**, not to calendar dates, so a slipping
   programme does not accelerate your payments.
2. **A bank guarantee or escrow over every payment made before the deed.** Ask explicitly; it is not
   offered by default.
3. **A longstop date with a financial consequence**, and clarity on whether that date can be extended
   without your consent.
4. **A specification schedule annexed to the contract**, because three years is long enough for
   materials and suppliers to change legitimately.
5. **Written confirmation of the tax treatment**, from your own lawyer, with the acknowledgement that it
   is assessed at deed date and may differ from the position today.
6. **The right to a [snagging inspection](/guides/snagging-inspection-greece-new-build/)** before final
   payment is released.

## Twenty owners is a small denominator for shared costs

A twenty-unit building has an owners' association like any other, and a much thinner budget to run it
with. Common charges on lifts, façade, roof, entrance and any shared plant are divided across twenty
millièmes rather than across sixty, so the per-unit figure is structurally higher than in a larger block
with the same equipment.

The exposure runs the other way too. One owner who stops paying is 5% of the annual budget, and on a
scheme sold substantially to non-resident buyers, collection is harder than it is in a building of
resident Athenians. Ask for the draft κανονισμός and the projected first-year common charges before
reservation, not after.

## Who this suits

A buyer whose objective is a central Athens apartment, who has the capital to leave committed for three
years, and who is treating the Golden Visa as a consequence of the purchase rather than its reason. The
long horizon suits somebody buying the asset.

It suits poorly a buyer who needs the permit on a timetable. Three years to a deed, then the Ministry
file after it, is a long way from a decision to a residence card, and there are schemes on this site
with handovers already past. Anyone in that position should read the
[Golden Visa timeline](/guides/golden-visa-greece-timeline-2026-realistic/) before committing capital to
a 2027 completion.
