## Competitive read

Who currently owns these SERPs: **globalcitizensolutions.com**, **astons.com / astonsgreece.com**,
**getgoldenvisa.com**, **globalresidenceindex.com**, **immigrantinvest.com**, **imidaily.com**,
**globalpropertyguide.com**, **tranio.com**, **investropa.com**, plus Greek law firms
(**varnavas.gr**, **avglaw.gr**) and **wfw.com**.

**What they do better:** named lawyer authorship with credentials; regularly updated primary-source citations;
full coverage of the non-property investment routes; the non-dom tax regimes.

**Where Greek Invest can beat them — and where these 50 aim:**

1. **Operational depth.** Competitors explain *what* the rule is. Almost nobody documents *how the file
   actually moves* — engineer certificates, cadastre extracts, bank traceability sequencing. Greek Invest
   already has this and under-uses it.
2. **The tax layer they skip.** Immigration sites cover the visa and stop. Property portals cover the
   purchase and stop. **Nobody joins Golden Visa → non-dom regime → exit tax** into one decision. That is
   the HNW buyer's actual question and the highest-value lead on the site.
3. **Greek-specific legal traps** — antiparochi, forest maps (δασικοί χάρτες), authadereta legalisation,
   e-auctions, Ktimatologio deadlines. Foreign-owned competitor sites do not cover these at all.
4. **Real numbers with dates and sources**, which the existing corpus already does well.

## Production standard (mandatory for all 50)

Every article must clear these before publish — several are the exact defects being cleaned out of the
existing corpus, and none may be reintroduced:

1. **Zero shared boilerplate.** No `MORE Group underwrites…`, no `Insider tip:` template, no `Planning line`
   table. Any cross-page block appears **once**, in one canonical location.
2. **Direct answer first.** ≤40 words after the H1, key figure in the first sentence.
3. **Title ≤60 characters including suffix**, or drop the suffix. No `::`, no repeated tokens.
4. **Meta description 140–155 characters**, leading with the number.
5. **5+ FAQs in frontmatter only** — never a second inline `FaqBlock` schema (see code Wave 1.1).
6. **Named author with credentials** for anything tax or legal (E-E-A-T; competitors already do this).
7. **Every figure dated and sourced** — "as of August 2026, per AADE/ELSTAT/Ministry of Migration".
8. **≥5 contextual internal links out, ≥3 planned inbound** before publish.
9. **One intent-matched `<InlineCta>`** mid-article — GV → GV consultation, yield/area → property
   consultation or calculator, project → shortlist.
10. **Unique hero with real `heroAlt`.** No further reuse of the Acropolis image (already on 9 pages).
11. **`relatedSlugs` populated.**
12. **Near-duplicate check:** <20% 5-gram containment against every existing page before merge.
